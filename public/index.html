Our services

What can Infinite Accounting do for you?

Infinite Accounting Services offers 2 core services in Orland Park. Many clients use both, because clean books make an accurate tax return much easier.

Bookkeeping services

We record and reconcile your transactions every month and give you reports you can read. Reconciling means matching every entry in your books to your bank and card statements.

  • Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliation
  • QuickBooks setup, clean-up and training
  • Catch-up work for months or years behind
  • Profit and loss statements and balance sheets

Tax preparation and tax help

  • Help with IRS and Illinois notices and late filings

Questions

What do people ask before they call us?

Don't see your question? Call or text (708) 232-3003 or read our full FAQ.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation with Infinite Accounting Services is free and there is no obligation. We look at where your books and taxes stand, answer your questions and explain what we would handle before any work begins.

Can you help if my books are months or years behind?

Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping is one of the most common reasons people call us. We work through your bank and card statements month by month, bring your records current and then help you keep them that way. See our bookkeeping services.

Do you work with QuickBooks?

Yes. We set up new QuickBooks files, clean up files that have become messy and train owners who want to handle some of the daily entries themselves. QuickBooks is accounting software made by Intuit that many small businesses use to track income and expenses.

Do you prepare both personal and business tax returns?

Yes. We prepare individual returns and business returns for sole proprietors, LLCs, partnerships and S corporations. Many owners like one firm to handle both because business income often flows onto the personal return. See our tax services.

What should I bring to my first meeting?

Bring what you have. That usually means last year's tax return, any tax forms you received this year, recent bank and credit card statements, and any letters from the IRS or the Illinois Department of Revenue. Disorganized records are fine. Sorting them out is part of our job.
