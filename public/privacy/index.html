Privacy policy for Infinite Accounting Services

Infinite Accounting Services Inc keeps the information you share with us private. This policy explains what we collect when you use this website or contact us, how we use it, how we protect your tax and financial information, and how you can stop text messages at any time.

Call (708) 232-3003 Contact us

Last updated October 14, 2026.

Who we are

This website belongs to Infinite Accounting Services Inc, a bookkeeping and tax firm at 14475 John Humphrey Dr, Ste 150, Orland Park, IL 60462. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Infinite Accounting Services Inc.

What information do we collect?

When you send the contact form or a chat message, we receive your name, your phone number and your message. When you call, text or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and what you tell us. If you become a client, we receive the financial, tax and identity information we need to do the work you hire us for.

This website does not use advertising cookies. The contact form and the chat are provided by our customer messaging service and may use cookies they need to work. If we add website analytics in the future, such as Google Analytics, it would record general information like the pages visited and the type of device, and we will update this policy first.

How do we use your information?

  • To reply to your inquiry and set up your free consultation.
  • To provide and bill for the services you hire us for.
  • To meet our legal and professional duties.
  • To keep this website working and secure.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your financial information for marketing.

Will we contact you by text message?

When you send the contact form or a chat message, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text or email about your inquiry. We only text you about your inquiry and the services you ask about. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP to any text, or by telling us. We do not share your phone number or your consent to receive texts with anyone for their marketing.

How do we protect your tax and financial information?

Tax and financial records are some of the most private information you have. We use reasonable safeguards to protect them, we limit access to the people who need it to serve you, and we keep client records confidential. Federal rules also limit how tax preparers may use and share tax return information.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, account numbers or tax documents through the website form or ordinary email. Once we are working together, we will agree on a safer way to share them. No way of sending or storing information is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Who do we share information with?

We share information only when we need to, for example with companies that host this website or provide our email, phone, text messaging, contact form and chat services, software we use for bookkeeping and tax work, and government agencies such as the IRS and the Illinois Department of Revenue when we file for you. We also share information when the law requires it, or when you ask us to.

What choices do you have?

  • You can call or text us instead of using the form or the chat.
  • You can ask us to stop calling, texting or emailing you.
  • You can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to correct it.
  • You can ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.

How long do we keep information?

We keep inquiry information as long as we need it to reply and follow up. We keep client records for the periods the law and professional standards require, then dispose of them securely.

Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 through this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

How can you reach us about privacy?

Call or text (708) 232-3003, email danielle@infiniteaccountingservices.com, or write to Infinite Accounting Services Inc, 14475 John Humphrey Dr, Ste 150, Orland Park, IL 60462. You can also read our terms of use.
