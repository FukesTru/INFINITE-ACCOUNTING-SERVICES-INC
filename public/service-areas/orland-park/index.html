How does Infinite Accounting help people in Orland Park?

Some clients like to put a face to the firm that handles their numbers, and that's easy to do here. Questions

What do Orland Park clients ask us?

Can I drop off my tax documents at the Orland Park office?

Yes, during office hours. Call or text (708) 232-3003 first so we can confirm a time and let you know what to bring. Many people like to hand over paperwork in person and follow up by phone. If anything is missing once we look through it, we'll tell you exactly what we still need instead of guessing.

What should I bring to a first meeting at your office?

Bring whatever you have. That means last year's tax return, recent bank and credit card statements, any letters from the IRS or the state, and your QuickBooks details if you use it. If your records are incomplete, bring them anyway. The free consultation is about understanding where things stand, not judging how organized you are.

Do you only work with businesses and families in Orland Park?

No. Our office is here, but we also work with clients in Tinley Park, Orland Hills, Palos Heights, Palos Hills, Frankfort, Crestwood and Worth. Location matters less than fit. If you want careful books, clear explanations and a small firm that knows your file, we'd be glad to talk.

What happens if I switch to you from another accountant?

We'll ask for your prior returns and access to your current books, review where things stand, and point out anything that needs attention before we take over. If the books need some clean-up first, we'll say so and explain why. Nothing changes until you decide to move forward, and your prior accountant's work stays part of your records.
