What do Orland Hills clients ask us?

Is it easier to work with an accountant close to home?

It depends on how you like to work. Some clients mostly check in by phone or text. Others like knowing they can stop by the office to drop off paperwork, sign forms or go over their return face to face. With our office in neighboring Orland Park, Orland Hills clients can choose whichever suits them.

I'm thinking about starting a business from home. When should I talk to an accountant?

Before your first sale, if possible. Early decisions, like whether to stay a sole proprietor, how to separate business and household money, and what records to keep, are easier to make at the start than to fix later. A free consultation is a good time to cover the basics and set up simple bookkeeping from day one.

My spouse runs a business from home. Can we still file jointly?

Yes. Married couples can generally file a joint return when one spouse has business income. The business is usually reported on its own schedule within the joint return, and self-employment tax applies to the spouse who runs it. We'll explain how the business affects your combined return and whether estimated payments make sense for you.

What records should I keep for a home-based business?

Keep records of income received, receipts for business purchases, mileage logs if you drive for work, and home expenses like utilities, rent or mortgage interest if you claim a home office. Bank and card statements help fill in the rest. We can suggest a simple filing system and keep the books current so nothing has to be rebuilt in a rush at tax time.
