Questions

What do Tinley Park clients ask us?

How far behind is too far behind for catch-up bookkeeping?

There isn't a real cutoff. We can work with records that are a few months behind or several years behind. The process is the same either way. We gather statements, reconcile month by month and correct anything that was recorded in the wrong place. The further back it goes, the longer it takes, so it helps to start well before the next filing deadline.

Should I catch up my books before or after I file my taxes?

Before, if you can. A business return is only as reliable as the numbers behind it, and estimating from bank deposits can lead to missed deductions or reporting errors. If a deadline is close, we can talk about filing an extension while the books are brought current. We'll walk through the tradeoffs with you during your free consultation.

Can you handle a restaurant with lots of small daily transactions?

Yes. High-volume businesses benefit from regular reconciliation more than most, because small errors repeat quickly. We match deposits to sales reports, record supplier invoices, and make sure card processing fees and payroll are accounted for. The monthly statements then show your sales and costs clearly enough to make decisions with.

I'm missing receipts from last year. Can you still help?

Usually, yes. Bank and credit card statements show most of the activity, and many vendors can reprint invoices on request. We'll point out which expenses need better documentation and which are already supported. Going forward, we'll suggest a simple habit for saving receipts. What we won't do is invent numbers to fill the gaps.
