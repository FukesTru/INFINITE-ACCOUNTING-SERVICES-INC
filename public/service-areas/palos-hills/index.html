Questions

What do Palos Hills clients ask us?

Do I have to report side income if I didn't get a 1099?

Yes. Income is generally taxable whether or not you receive a form for it. A 1099 is just one way a payer reports what they sent you. We help you add up income from every source, including cash and app payments, and report it alongside the expenses you're allowed to deduct.

What counts as a deductible expense for a side business?

Generally, costs that are ordinary and necessary for the work, such as supplies, software, part of your phone bill, or business mileage. Personal costs don't count, and mixed-use items need a reasonable split. The rules can be specific, so we review your expenses with you, explain which ones qualify and why, and help you keep records that support them.

I'm a student with a part-time freelance gig. Do I need to file a return?

It depends on your total income and how much came from self-employment. Self-employment income has its own, much lower filing threshold, so even a small gig can mean you need to file. Your parents' return may also be affected if they claim you as a dependent. We can look at your numbers and explain what applies to you.

Should I form an LLC for my freelance work?

Not necessarily. An LLC can make sense for some freelancers, but on its own it doesn't change how your income is taxed, and it adds state filing requirements. Many freelancers operate as sole proprietors with good records. We can talk through the tax side of the decision, and we'd suggest an attorney for the legal side.
