Questions

What do Worth clients ask us?

Why is my refund smaller this year than last year?

There are several common reasons. It could be a change in your withholding, a credit you no longer qualify for, a child aging out of a dependent credit, or extra income such as interest or a side job. We compare this year's return with last year's and explain the difference in plain terms, so you know what changed and whether to adjust your withholding.

What does 'reconciling' my accounts actually mean?

It means checking that every transaction in your books matches what your bank and credit card statements show, and finding anything that's missing, duplicated or recorded wrong. It's the step that makes your financial reports trustworthy. We do it monthly for bookkeeping clients and explain anything we find in simple language.

Do I really need a bookkeeper for a very small business?

Not always. An owner with only a handful of transactions a month can often keep their own records with a simple spreadsheet. Others find that a little monthly help makes tax season much easier. We'll look at your volume and tell you honestly which option makes sense, even if the answer is handling it yourself.

What should I do if I get a letter from the IRS or the state?

Don't ignore it, and try not to panic. Many notices are routine requests for information or proposed adjustments with a clear response deadline. Note the date and any amount listed, then call or text us. We'll help you understand what the letter is asking, gather the documents involved, and prepare a response if one is needed.
