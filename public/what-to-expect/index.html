What to expect at your free consultation

Your first consultation with Infinite Accounting Services in Orland Park is free and has no obligation. We talk through your books, your taxes or the letter you received, look at what you bring, and explain what we would handle and what it costs before any work begins.

Step by step

How does the first consultation work?

  1. You reach out

    Call or text (708) 232-3003, email us or send the form. We reply to set a time that works for you.

  2. We talk

    You tell us about your business or household, what is worrying you and what you would like to change.

  3. We review

    We look at what you bring, such as returns, statements, your QuickBooks file or a notice.

  4. You decide

    We explain our recommendation and the fee. You decide whether to go ahead, with no pressure.

Checklist

What should you bring to your first meeting?

Bring what applies to you. If something is missing, bring what you have and we will help fill the gaps.

Individuals and families

  • Last year's federal and Illinois tax returns
  • W-2s and any 1099 forms you received
  • Mortgage interest and property tax statements
  • Child care, education and charitable donation records
  • Any letters from the IRS or the Illinois Department of Revenue

Business owners

  • Last year's business and personal returns
  • Bank and credit card statements for the business
  • Your QuickBooks login or other bookkeeping files
  • Payroll reports, if you have employees
  • Your EIN letter and any K-1 forms

An EIN is the federal tax ID number for a business. A K-1 is the form that reports your share of income from a partnership or S corporation.

Questions about your first consultation

Is there any cost or obligation for the first consultation?

No. The first consultation with Infinite Accounting Services is free, and there is no obligation to hire us afterward.

What if my records are a mess?

That is fine and very common. Bring what you have, even if it is a box of receipts. Sorting records out is part of our work, and we will tell you exactly what else we need.

Will I know the fee before you start?

Yes. We explain what we would handle and what it costs before any work begins. Nothing starts until you agree.
