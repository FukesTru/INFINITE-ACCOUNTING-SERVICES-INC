About Infinite Accounting Services

Meet Danielle Douglas, our President

Danielle Douglas is the President of Infinite Accounting Services, a bookkeeping and tax firm in Orland Park, Illinois. She founded the firm in 2018 to give small business owners, independent professionals and families an accountant who explains their numbers in plain English and answers the phone.

Call (708) 232-3003 Contact us

Serving clients in the southwest suburbs since 2018

Her story

How did Danielle get started in accounting?

Danielle's path into accounting began close to home. Family members with small businesses asked her for help keeping their business bank accounts in order. She saw how much a little clarity could change things for an owner, and helping family turned into a career.

Danielle has worked in the accounting industry for about 20 years. Along the way she earned an MBA, which broadened her view of how bookkeeping, taxes and business decisions fit together.

In 2018 she founded Infinite Accounting Services in the southwest suburbs. The idea was to offer what many small business owners and families could not find at larger firms. That means one person who knows their file, keeps their records accurate and takes the time to explain their numbers.

Her guiding belief is simple. Clients should understand their own reports and returns. When you know where you stand, you make better decisions for your business and your family.

How the firm works

What can you expect from working with Danielle?

Education first

We explain what your numbers mean and why they matter, so you can make decisions with confidence.

Accuracy and detail

Every account is reconciled and every figure is checked before anything is filed or sent.

Discretion

Your documents and conversations stay confidential and are shared only with people you authorize.

Responsiveness

Questions do not wait for April. You can call, text or email and expect a real answer.

Who we work with

Who does Infinite Accounting Services work with?

Our clients range from owner-run shops to busy executives. What they share is a preference for personal service.

Small and mid-sized business owners

Shops, trades, service companies and growing LLCs that need steady books and dependable tax preparation.

Executives

Busy professionals who want their personal returns handled carefully and discreetly.

Independent professionals

Consultants, freelancers and self-employed people with 1099 income. A 1099 is the tax form a business sends when it pays you as a contractor.

Individuals and families

Households that want their taxes done right and explained clearly, year after year.
