What affects the price

Why does the price change from one client to the next?

Accounts

The number of bank, credit card and loan accounts we reconcile each month changes the time involved.

Transactions

A business with more sales, bills and payments each month needs more bookkeeping time.

How far behind

Catch-up work is priced by how many months or years need to be brought current.

Type of return

A business return for a partnership or S corporation takes more work than a simple personal return.

Questions

Questions about fees

Why don't you list one flat price for everyone?

Because the work is different for every client. A business with 1 bank account and 40 transactions a month needs far less time than one with 6 accounts and 600 transactions. We quote after we see your situation, so the price fits the work.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first consultation is free and has no obligation.

Will the price change without warning?

No. The fee is agreed in your engagement letter before work begins. If your needs change, we talk with you first.
