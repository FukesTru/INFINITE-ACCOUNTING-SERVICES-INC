Bespoke Services

Bespoke Services for healthcare and mental health practices

Bespoke Services are tailored bookkeeping engagements from Infinite Accounting Services, a bookkeeping and tax firm in Orland Park, Illinois, for healthcare and mental health practices nationwide. Each engagement starts with a bookkeeping cleanup, then moves to ongoing monthly accounting shaped around how your practice runs. They are built for practices earning $1 million or more a year.

The engagement

What does a bespoke engagement include?

Every Bespoke Services engagement has 2 parts. First we put your practice's books right. Then we keep them right, month after month.

Part 1

Bookkeeping cleanup

For practices whose books are behind, unreconciled or no longer trusted.

  • Missing transactions gathered, sorted and categorized
  • Every bank and credit card account reconciled, period by period
  • Your QuickBooks file corrected, with a clean chart of accounts
  • Accurate financial statements once the cleanup is done
Book a call about a cleanup
Part 2

Ongoing monthly accounting

For practices that want clean books every month, kept by a firm that already knows them.

  • Every transaction recorded and every account reconciled each month
  • A monthly profit and loss statement and balance sheet
  • Cash flow reporting, with receivables and payables kept current
  • Books ready for your business and personal tax returns
Book a call about monthly accounting
Built around your practice

How is each engagement tailored to your practice?

No two practices keep their books the same way. Before any work begins, we agree on how your engagement fits your practice and write it into your engagement letter, the signed agreement that sets out the work and the fee.

Your schedule

Monthly bookkeeping for most practices, or quarterly when a practice has fewer transactions.

Your software

We work in QuickBooks. If your practice uses other software, we decide together whether to keep it or move.

Your structure

Separate, consistent books for each location or company you own.

Your team

Pay for staff and clinicians recorded correctly, whether they are employees or contractors.

Your reports

Monthly statements and cash flow reporting, explained in plain English by someone who knows your file.

Your scope and fee

Quoted after we review your books, then set in your engagement letter before any work begins.

Who it is for

Is a bespoke engagement right for your practice?

Bespoke Services are designed for established practices earning $1 million or more a year. At that size, accurate books shape payroll, partner decisions, lending and taxes.

It is likely a good fit if

  • Your practice earns $1 million or more a year
  • You run a medical, specialty, mental health, dental or allied health practice
  • Your books are behind, or you want them kept to a higher standard
  • You want one firm for your books and your tax returns
  • You would rather work with a small, owner-run firm than a large one

Questions

What do practice owners ask about Bespoke Services?

Don't see your question? Call or text (708) 232-3003 or read our full FAQ.

What makes Bespoke Services different from standard bookkeeping?

A Bespoke Services engagement is scoped around your practice. We review your books first, then agree on the cleanup, the monthly schedule, the reports and the fee before any work begins. You can also read about our bookkeeping services.

Do you work with practices in other states?

Yes. Infinite Accounting Services works with healthcare and mental health practices across the United States. Calls are by phone or video, and we work from your statements and accounting software online.

Does my practice need to earn $1 million a year?

Bespoke Services are designed for practices earning $1 million or more a year. If your practice is smaller, book a call anyway and we will tell you plainly which of our services fits.

What happens after the cleanup?

Once your books are current, the engagement moves to ongoing monthly accounting. Each month we record and reconcile your accounts and send your statements, so your books stay current.

How is the fee for a bespoke engagement set?

The fee depends on the size of the cleanup and on how many accounts and transactions your practice has each month. We quote it after reviewing your books, and it is written into your engagement letter before any work begins.

Will you need my patients' health information?

Bookkeeping works from bank and card statements, payroll records and your accounting software. Please never send patient names, health information or Social Security numbers through the website form or by email.
