Why it matters

Why do accurate books matter for your business?

When your books are accurate and current, you can see which months are strong, where costs are creeping up and how much cash you really have. That makes everyday decisions easier.

Accurate books also make tax season calmer. Your income and expenses are already organized and reconciled, so your return is faster to prepare and more reliable.

And when you apply for a loan, a lease or a line of credit, lenders ask for financial statements. Current books show them a business that is organized.

With clean books you can

  • Make decisions from real numbers
  • Walk into tax season prepared
  • Hand lenders statements they can rely on
  • Spot problems early, before they grow
  • Spend evenings on your business instead of paperwork

Questions

Bookkeeping questions, answered

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?

Bookkeeping is the day to day recording, sorting and reconciling of your transactions. Accounting builds on those records to produce reports, plan ahead and prepare tax returns. Accurate bookkeeping makes everything that depends on it more reliable.

How often will you update my books?

Most ongoing clients choose monthly bookkeeping. Each month we record your transactions, reconcile your accounts and send reports you can review. Businesses with fewer transactions sometimes choose a quarterly schedule.

Do you work in QuickBooks, or can I keep my software?

We work in QuickBooks and can set up a new file or clean up an existing one. If you use different software, tell us what you have. We will talk through whether to keep it or move, based on what keeps your records accurate.

Can you catch up several years of bookkeeping?

Yes. We regularly help owners who are months or years behind. We work through statements period by period, reconcile each account and bring your books current. If tax returns are also unfiled, we coordinate both.

Is my financial information kept confidential?

Yes. Your records are used only to provide the services you engage us for, and they stay within our small firm. Please never send Social Security or full account numbers through the website form.
