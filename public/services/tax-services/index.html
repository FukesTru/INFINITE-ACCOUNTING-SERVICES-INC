Tax season checklist

What should you bring to your tax appointment?

Gather what applies to you. Missing something? Bring what you have and we will help fill the gaps.

Individuals and families

  • Last year's federal and Illinois tax returns
  • Photo ID, plus Social Security numbers and birth dates for everyone on the return
  • W-2s from every employer
  • 1099 forms for interest, dividends, retirement or freelance income
  • Mortgage interest statements and property tax bills
  • Child care costs and provider information
  • Tuition statements and student loan interest
  • Records of charitable donations
  • Estimated tax payments you made
  • Letters from the IRS or the Illinois Department of Revenue

Business owners

  • Last year's business and personal returns
  • Profit and loss statement and balance sheet, or bank and card statements for the year
  • 1099s you received and records of 1099s you sent
  • Payroll reports, if you have employees
  • Records of equipment, vehicle and other large purchases
  • Vehicle mileage log and home office details, if they apply
  • Business loan statements
  • Estimated tax payments for the business or owners
  • Your EIN letter and any K-1 forms
  • Sales tax filings, if you collect sales tax

Business owners can read our guide to the records you need to prepare your business taxes.

Questions

Tax preparation questions

What documents do I need for my tax appointment?

Start with last year's return, every income form you received, such as W-2s and 1099s, and records of deductible expenses. Business owners should add their profit and loss statement and balance sheet, or bank statements for the year. The checklist on this page covers the essentials.

Can you prepare both my personal and business returns?

Yes, and many owners prefer it. Business income often flows onto your personal return, so one firm preparing both keeps everything consistent. If we also keep your books, your business return starts from reconciled numbers.

I received a letter from the IRS. Can you help?

Yes. Many notices simply ask for information or point out a mismatch. We help you understand the letter, compare it with your return, gather documents and prepare a response before the deadline. Read what to do first after an IRS notice.

Can you file back taxes for years I missed?

Yes. We work out which years are needed, gather the income records for each one and prepare the returns. Getting caught up is usually better sooner than later.
