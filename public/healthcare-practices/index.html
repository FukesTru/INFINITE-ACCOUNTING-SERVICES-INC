Our services

How do we help healthcare and mental health practices?

Start with a cleanup, then move to monthly accounting so your books stay current. Or choose either one on its own.

Bookkeeping cleanup

A bookkeeping cleanup is a one-time project that corrects and completes your past records. We work through every month that is behind or wrong until your books are accurate and current.

  • Every bank, credit card and loan account reconciled to its statements
  • Miscategorized and duplicate entries corrected
  • Owner draws and personal charges separated from practice expenses
  • QuickBooks categories set up to fit how your practice earns and spends
  • Books brought current and ready for your tax preparer
Ask about a cleanup

Monthly accounting

Once your books are clean, we keep them that way. Each month we record and reconcile every transaction and send financial statements. A profit and loss statement shows what the practice earned and spent, and a balance sheet shows what it owns and owes.

  • Transactions recorded and categorized every month
  • Bank, card and loan accounts reconciled monthly
  • Monthly profit and loss statement and balance sheet
  • Cash flow reporting so you can plan ahead
  • Accounts payable and receivable tracked

Questions

Healthcare practice bookkeeping questions, answered

Do you work with practices outside Illinois?

Yes. Infinite Accounting Services works with healthcare and mental health practices anywhere in the United States. We work by phone, text and email, and our office is in Orland Park, Illinois.

Which kinds of practices is this service for?

This service is for healthcare and mental health practices, such as medical, dental, chiropractic and physical therapy offices, and therapy, counseling and psychiatry group practices. Our focus is practices with $1 million or more in yearly revenue.

What is a bookkeeping cleanup?

A bookkeeping cleanup is a one-time project that corrects and completes past records. We reconcile each account to its statements, fix entries in the wrong categories, record what is missing and bring the books current. Monthly accounting then keeps them that way.

Do you need our patient or client records?

No. Your books are kept from bank and card statements, deposit totals, bills and your accounting software. Please do not send patient or client health information through the website form.

Do you work in QuickBooks?

Yes. We set up new QuickBooks files and clean up existing ones. If your practice uses different software, tell us what you have, and we will talk through whether to keep it or move.

How much does bookkeeping for a practice cost?

Every practice is different. The fee depends on how many accounts and transactions you have and how far behind the books are. We quote after your free consultation, and the agreed fee is written in your engagement letter before any work begins.
