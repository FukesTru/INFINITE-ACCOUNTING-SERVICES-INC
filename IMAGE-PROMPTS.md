**To add one:**

1. Download the source URL.
2. Convert it to WebP at about 1600px wide (2400px for a full-width band), under 300 KB.
3. Save it as `public/images/<file>.webp`.
4. Place it with real `width` and `height`, specific alt text and `loading="lazy"` (unless it is above the fold).
5. Add it to `site.json` `images` with a note saying it is AI-generated.
